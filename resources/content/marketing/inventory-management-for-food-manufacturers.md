Inventory management for food manufacturers is more than a stock number. Small teams need to know what is on hand, what is being purchased, what is being made, and what sales orders may consume.

FooMake connects materials, supplier packs, purchase orders, inventory counts, and production so inventory work can live close to the actual manufacturing workflow.

## The problem

Inventory gets messy when counts, supplier packs, and production planning live apart. A team may know a material exists, but not whether the current stock is enough for tomorrow's make order or next week's customer demand.

## How FooMake helps

FooMake supports practical stock workflows:

- Materials with units of measure
- Supplier package options
- Purchase orders and receiving
- Inventory counts
- Make orders and production demand
- Sales orders where demand applies

## Counts that support real work

Inventory counts help teams correct stock and keep trust in the system. FooMake treats counts as workflow records so assigned users can see the work they need to do.

## Purchasing and production context

Inventory should not be isolated from buying and making. FooMake keeps purchase order and make order context nearby so small food manufacturers can understand what is available now and what is expected soon.

Learn more about [purchase order software for food manufacturers](/learn/purchase-order-software-for-food-manufacturers), [production planning](/learn/production-planning-for-small-food-manufacturers), and [food manufacturing MRP](/learn/food-manufacturing-mrp).

## Why not spreadsheets?

Spreadsheets can track a count, but they do not naturally enforce units, supplier pack conversions, purchase order status, or workflow ownership. FooMake is built to keep those parts connected.
