MRP for small manufacturers should be practical. Teams need clearer materials, purchasing, inventory, production, and sales order workflows without committing to a heavy enterprise ERP rollout.

FooMake is simple MRP software for small food manufacturers and small-batch producers who have outgrown spreadsheets.

## The problem

Small manufacturers often run production from a mix of spreadsheets, notes, supplier emails, and memory. That works until more products, more suppliers, more orders, and more assigned work create too much friction.

## How FooMake helps

FooMake brings core manufacturing workflows together:

- Materials
- Suppliers
- Purchase orders
- Recipes
- Make orders
- Inventory counts
- Sales orders
- Tasks and workflows where available

## Built for a focused rollout

FooMake is intentionally smaller than enterprise ERP. It is built around daily workflows and early access adoption, with active improvements shaped by practical production needs.

## Why not spreadsheets?

Spreadsheets are easy to start and hard to control. FooMake gives small manufacturers a clearer operational structure while staying focused on the work that matters.

## FAQ

### Is FooMake a full ERP?

FooMake is a focused MRP system, not a heavy enterprise ERP suite.

### Is FooMake useful for small teams?

Yes. FooMake is built for small-batch teams that need practical production visibility.

### Can FooMake replace spreadsheets?

FooMake can replace many operational spreadsheets for materials, recipes, purchasing, inventory, production, and sales order work.
