Food manufacturing MRP should help a small team run the day, not bury it in enterprise process. FooMake is simple MRP software for small food manufacturers who have outgrown spreadsheets and need one place for materials, recipes, purchasing, production, inventory counts, sales orders, and assigned work.

FooMake is in beta, ready for companies to use, and actively improved around real small-batch production workflows.

## The problem with spreadsheet MRP

Spreadsheets are flexible, but they become fragile as production gets busier. Ingredient lists, supplier pack sizes, purchase orders, counts, make orders, and customer demand all start living in separate files.

That makes it hard to answer practical questions:

- Do we have enough material for tomorrow's run?
- Which supplier pack should we buy?
- Which recipe version is being made?
- Which counts and production tasks are assigned today?
- Which sales orders are creating demand?

## How FooMake helps

FooMake connects the operational pieces small food makers already manage:

- Materials and units of measure
- Supplier packs and purchase orders
- Recipes and make orders
- Inventory counts and stock visibility
- Sales orders and workflow tasks where available

## Built for food production workflows

Food makers deal with recipes, pack sizes, conversions, supplier realities, and production timing. FooMake keeps those workflows close together so the team can move from planning to purchasing to making without rebuilding the same numbers in every spreadsheet.

## Why not enterprise ERP?

Large ERP systems can be powerful, but they often assume a large implementation team, heavy configuration, and long rollout cycles. FooMake is intentionally smaller: it focuses on the workflows that matter first for small-batch food manufacturers.
